# 13. What to Avoid

Raising marks is half cutting leaks.

## Study leaks

| Avoid | Do instead |
|-------|------------|
| Passive re-reading / mass highlighting | Active recall, past questions |
| Cram-only lifestyle | Spacing + weekly papers |
| Phone on the desk | Phone in another room |
| “Study” chats all evening | Timed co-study, then free chat |
| Notes you never test | Q&A + self-tests |
| Skipping mark-scheme analysis | Error log |
| Only easy topics | Interleave hard topics when energy is high |
| All-nighters | Sleep as performance gear |

## Mindset leaks

- “I am my rank”  
- Waiting for perfect mood/plan  
- Manifestation without practice  
- “Everyone else has it figured out”  
- Revenge bedtime  
- Comparison addiction  

## Social leaks

- Mocking people who try  
- Answer-harvesting that skips understanding  
- Ranking friends for drama  
- Dragging allies into late nights before papers  
- Full-time romance/drama in peak exam windows  
- Effort-hiding so others underperform with you  

## Home & digital leaks

- Fights right before deep work  
- Studying in the loudest room “to prove toughness”  
- Unlimited short video between blocks  
- “Just one episode” with no hard stop  
- Posting study aesthetics instead of doing problems  

## Red-flag audit (2+ true → cut this week)

- [ ] More talk about grades than timed practice  
- [ ] Know friends’ scores better than own weak topics  
- [ ] Studied with notifications on  
- [ ] Under 6 hours sleep more than twice  
- [ ] Avoided one hard subject all week  
- [ ] Sessions started late because of chat/video
