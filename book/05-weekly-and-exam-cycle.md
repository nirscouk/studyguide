# 5. Weekly & Exam Cycle Routine

A steady weekly rhythm beats last-minute sprints.

## Default week

| Day | Focus |
|-----|--------|
| **Mon–Thu** | Learn new material + daily active recall |
| **Friday** | Review the week + mixed practice questions |
| **Weekend** | Longer practice tests *or* deep work on weak areas + light review |

## Pre-exam shift (1–2 weeks out)

- Heavy emphasis on **past papers** under **timed conditions**  
- **Spaced review** of weak topics from the error log  
- **Reduce new learning** — consolidate what will be tested  

## Progress tracking (simple)

After each study session, write:

1. What you covered  
2. One thing that still feels shaky  
3. Next action (re-test date or follow-up problem set)  

```text
Session log — ____ / ____ / ____
Covered:
Still shaky:
Next action:
Minutes of true focus (not hours “at desk”):
```

Measure **output and retention**, not time parked at a desk.
